# Emirati property concept: Dar Concept

**Status:** editable fictional design study; synthetic render validation only.
This is not a property listing, architectural plan, investment recommendation
or verified Emirati voice sample.

**Audience and objective:** Emirati viewers considering what they want from a
home. Introduce an imagined two-bedroom home, explain its open living area,
and invite viewers to identify their own priorities before a future visit.
There is no address, seller, booking link, price, floor area or return claim.

**Source:** `configs/emirati.json` owns the Arabic draft narration and captions.
Use one ready `ae` catalog voice only after an audition and qualified Emirati
review. The intended delivery is calm and welcoming. The label alone does not
prove a voice's regional suitability.

The exterior, schematic plan, room and doorway logo are original editable SVG
shapes. Sand, stone and muted green replace the other examples' palettes.
The drawings are conceptual and do not document a built or available home.
The on-screen fictional-home notice must remain even after narration is approved.

**Timing:** three measured scenes at 30 fps with a four-second minimum visual
hold, 0.2-second lead and 0.4-second tail around each complete WAV. The silent
layout preview is 12 seconds; actual speech determines the final duration.
No music, property footage, paid render service or generated photography is used.

**Before publication:** obtain qualified Emirati wording/listening review,
verify the full recording against each script, and inspect all scene boundaries
and captions in the final MP4 on a phone. A real listing would require approved
real assets and factual details supplied by its owner; none are implied here.
Preserve the source configs, SVGs, accepted WAVs and props for further editing.
