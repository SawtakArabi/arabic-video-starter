# Egyptian Cairene explainer: Khotwa

**Status:** editable creative draft; synthetic render validation only. No
qualified Cairene narration review or real planner product is represented.

**Audience and objective:** busy Egyptian students or working adults with a
long task list. Explain a concrete routine: select one priority, divide it into
small scheduled steps, then review what was completed. The viewer can try the
routine on paper; no app registration, subscription or feature claim is needed.

**Source:** `configs/egyptian.json` owns the Arabic draft narration and independent
display copy. Select one actual ready `eg-cairene` voice and review an audition
before generating all scenes. Aim for a conversational explanation, not a sales
pitch. Khotwa is an invented demonstration identity.

All illustrations and the step-mark logo are original editable SVG shapes.
Blue-gray paper colors and a rust accent distinguish this lesson from the mug
ad. The drawings show a paper routine, not screenshots or a working application.
On-screen text is deliberately shorter than the spoken explanation and is not
presented as synchronized subtitles.

**Timing:** three scenes at 30 fps, at least four seconds each, with measured
WAV duration plus 0.2-second lead and 0.4-second tail. The silent preview is
12 seconds; real narration can make it longer. No music or sound effects mask
the instructions. Use accepted full WAVs; do not speed them up to fit a target.

**Before publication:** have a qualified Cairene speaker review wording,
pronunciation and pacing, check each actual recording against its script, and
confirm the final illustrations/captions remain readable on a phone. Keep the
synthetic preview visibly labeled until replaced. No paid take or listening
approval is included in this example.
